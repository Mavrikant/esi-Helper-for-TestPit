## Outline

The **Outline** view (Explorer side bar) lists the sections of the active `.esi` file — the step list, each `[STEP n]` with its inputs and outputs, definitions, variables, includes and component blocks.

The same structure drives:

- breadcrumbs above the editor,
- **Go to Symbol in Editor** (`Ctrl+Shift+O`),
- folding of sections.

**ESI Helper: Go to Matching Tag** (`Ctrl+Shift+\`) jumps between an opening tag and its closing tag.
