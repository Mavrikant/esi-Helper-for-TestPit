## Step picker

With a `.esi` file open, press `Ctrl+G` (**ESI Helper: Go to Step**).

- The list shows every `[STEP n]` section with its description.
- Type a step number, or text from a step description, to filter.
- Moving through the list previews the step in the editor; `Enter` jumps, `Esc` returns to where you were.

In `.esi` files this replaces the built-in *Go to Line* on `Ctrl+G`; *Go to Line* remains available from the Command Palette.

Related, in the editor context menu: **Update Step Numbers** renumbers the numbered steps (10, 20, 30, …) in document order, and **Refactor Document** trims trailing whitespace and turns tabs into four spaces.
