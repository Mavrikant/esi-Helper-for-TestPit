## Quick fixes

Many `esi Helper` problems carry a fix. Put the cursor on the squiggle and press `Ctrl+.`, or click the light bulb.

Examples:

- add a missing closing tag, or correct a closing tag that does not match its opener,
- replace a misspelled component, field or enum name with the closest name from the active profile,
- renumber the steps,
- *Fix all … problems in this file* for a whole category at once.

Findings reported by `TestPit.exe` (source `TestPit`) have no quick fix.

Every fix is an ordinary edit: review it, and undo it with `Ctrl+Z` if it is not what you meant.
